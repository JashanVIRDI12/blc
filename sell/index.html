    Questionsowners ask.

    Do you buy cars outside Delhi?

    Yes. We buy and sell across India. Tell us where the car is, and we’ll talk about how to see it.

    How is the offer decided?

    By the car itself: its specification, condition, kilometres, ownership and service history, and what cars like it are selling for. We explain the offer in person.

    My car is still on a loan. Can I sell it?

    Yes. The loan has to be closed and the bank’s NOC issued before the RC can be transferred. Mention it when you share the details, and we’ll talk you through it.

    Can I exchange it for one of your cars?

    Yes. Choose the exchange in the form, and your car’s value can go towards one from our collection, or one our concierge finds for you.

    Who handles the transfer?

    We prepare the transfer papers, Forms 29 and 30, with you, and the RC moves to the new owner through the RTO.
