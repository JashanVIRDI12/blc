Two founders, one promise.

Vinod Ahuja and Pawan Ahuja founded Baba Luxury Cars more than eighteen years ago, on a promise that still holds.

Their first sale was a Hyundai Santro, listed online when few dealers in Delhi did that, and sold on one promise: that the car would be exactly as it was described.

One car became two, then a small selection, and in time a showroom in Paschim Vihar. What never changed was how each deal was done: face to face, with the care we would want if we were the ones sitting across the desk.

Today there are more than fifty luxury cars to choose from, each one chosen and checked by us before it is listed. The promise has not changed.
